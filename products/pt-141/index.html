Bremelanotide (Melanocortin Receptor Agonist)

Scientific Overview

PT-141 (bremelanotide) is a synthetic cyclic melanocortin-receptor agonist developed from the melanotan peptide family. It is studied for central melanocortin pathways involved in sexual desire and arousal signaling.

Mechanism focus: Acts primarily via melanocortin receptors (notably MC4R-related pathways) in central nervous system circuits. Clinical literature focuses on hypoactive sexual desire disorder research contexts.
