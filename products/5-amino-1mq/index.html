5-Amino-1MQ

NNMT Inhibitor

Scientific Overview

5-Amino-1MQ is a quinolinium small molecule studied as an inhibitor of nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT). It is not a peptide. Laboratory work examines how blocking NNMT changes nicotinamide methylation and related metabolic readouts in cell and animal models.

Mechanism focus: NNMT methylates nicotinamide and consumes cellular methyl donor. Inhibitor studies, including work that names 5-amino-1-methylquinolinium, follow that enzyme in adipose and other metabolic models. Findings stay preclinical relative to a settled human mechanism.
