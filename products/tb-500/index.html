TB-500

Thymosin Beta-4 Fragment (LKKTETQ motif)

Scientific Overview

TB-500 refers to a research fragment centered on the actin-binding motif of thymosin β4 (Tβ4). Full-length Tβ4 is a 43–amino-acid peptide that sequesters G-actin and participates in cell migration and tissue remodeling pathways.

Mechanism focus: Thymosin β4 regulates actin polymerization, cell motility, and regenerative signaling. The LKKTETQ motif is a key actin-binding region studied for wound and soft-tissue repair biology.

Research Use Cases

  • Actin dynamics and cell-migration assays
  • Wound-healing and dermal repair models
  • Cardiac and neurological injury research using Tβ4 biology
  • Comparative studies of Tβ4 fragments versus full-length peptide

Use-Case Study Notations

  • Case-study notation: regenerative reviews summarize Tβ4 activity across dermal, ocular, and cardiac injury models, emphasizing actin-mediated cell migration.
  • Case-study notation: neurological injury literature explores Tβ4 as a restorative peptide candidate in stroke and neurodegeneration models.
  • Commercial “TB-500” preparations should be analytically distinguished from full-length thymosin β4.
