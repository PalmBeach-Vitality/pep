Scientific Overview

CJC-1295 is a growth hormone–releasing hormone (GHRH) analog family. DAC (Drug Affinity Complex) versions bind albumin for multi-day GH/IGF-1 elevation; no-DAC material — also called Modified GRF (1-29) — is shorter-acting and is what the live .store listing currently sells.

Mechanism focus: Stimulates pituitary GHRH receptors. DAC chemistry extends half-life; no-DAC lots should not be treated as interchangeable with DAC lots without a matching COA.

Individual purchases currently map to CJC Vial on .store: CJC (no DAC), 100 mg in a 10 mL liquid vial. A DAC SKU is not listed. The selected studies below primarily describe DAC CJC-1295 pharmacokinetics.
