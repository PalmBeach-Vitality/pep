CJC-1295 (DAC)

Long-Acting GHRH Analog

Scientific Overview

CJC-1295 is a long-acting growth hormone–releasing hormone (GHRH) analog designed for prolonged GH and IGF-1 elevation via albumin binding (DAC versions).

Mechanism focus: Stimulates pituitary GHRH receptors; DAC chemistry extends half-life, producing multi-day GH/IGF-1 elevation in human pharmacokinetic studies.

Research Use Cases

  • Long-acting GHRH pharmacology
  • GH/IGF-1 axis kinetics
  • Serum proteome response to sustained GH drive
  • Anti-doping detection of GHRH analogs

Use-Case Study Notations

  • Case-study notation: clinical pharmacology papers showed prolonged GH and IGF-1 secretion after CJC-1295.
  • Case-study notation: once-daily dosing studies quantified enhanced GH/IGF-1 profiles versus shorter GHRH fragments.
  • Specify DAC vs non-DAC material in documentation—half-life differs substantially.
