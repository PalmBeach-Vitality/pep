Ipamorelin

Selective Growth Hormone Secretagogue

Scientific Overview

Ipamorelin is a selective ghrelin-receptor (GHS-R1a) agonist developed as a growth-hormone secretagogue with reduced ACTH/cortisol off-target activity relative to earlier GHRPs.

Mechanism focus: Binds GHS-R1a to stimulate pituitary GH release in a pulse-compatible manner; studied for nitrogen balance, bone, and GI motility endpoints in animals.
