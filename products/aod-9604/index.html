AOD-9604

hGH Fragment 176–191 Analog

Scientific Overview

AOD-9604 is a synthetic peptide corresponding to the C-terminal lipolytic domain of human growth hormone (fragment 176–191), studied for fat-metabolism effects without classical GH growth activity.

Mechanism focus: Investigated for β3-adrenergic–related lipolytic signaling and adipose metabolic regulation distinct from full-length GH IGF-1 growth pathways.

Research Use Cases

  • Adipose lipid-metabolism assays
  • Obesity pharmacology screening
  • Cartilage/OA intra-articular exploratory models
  • Anti-doping detection method development

Use-Case Study Notations

  • Case-study notation: early metabolic studies in obese mouse models evaluated lipid metabolism after chronic AOD9604 exposure.
  • Case-study notation: rabbit osteoarthritis work explored intra-articular AOD9604 ± hyaluronic acid.
  • Human efficacy evidence is more limited than for GLP-1 class agents; emphasize preclinical framing.
