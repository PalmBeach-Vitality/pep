Melanotan II

Melanocortin Receptor Agonist

Scientific Overview

Melanotan II is a synthetic cyclic peptide that binds melanocortin receptors in pharmacological research. This page covers receptor and laboratory signaling literature.

Mechanism focus: The cyclic lactam is studied for melanocortin-receptor affinity, including MC4R-related pathways. Published animal work examines central melanocortin signaling, feeding behavior, and thermogenic models.
