Scientific Overview

CJC (no DAC)/Ipamorelin pairs a short-acting GHRH analog with a selective ghrelin-mimetic secretagogue. The combination is widely referenced in research settings examining dual GH-axis inputs.

Mechanism focus: GHRH-receptor stimulation (CJC/Mod GRF) plus selective GHS-R1a activation (ipamorelin). Scientific support is drawn from component GHRH-analog and ipamorelin literature.
