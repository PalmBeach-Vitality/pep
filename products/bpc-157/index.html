BPC-157

Body Protection Compound-157

Scientific Overview

BPC-157 is a synthetic 15–amino-acid fragment derived from a protective protein sequence found in human gastric juice. In laboratory models it is studied for effects on soft-tissue repair pathways, angiogenesis, and gastrointestinal mucosal integrity.

Mechanism focus: Preclinical work suggests BPC-157 may influence nitric-oxide signaling, growth-factor pathways (including VEGFR2-related angiogenesis), and tendon/muscle fibroblast activity. Most mechanistic data remain preclinical.

Research Use Cases

  • Tendon and ligament healing models (transection and crush injury designs)
  • Skeletal-muscle repair and functional recovery studies
  • Gastrointestinal ulcer and fistula healing models
  • Vascular and angiogenesis pathway research

Use-Case Study Notations

  • Case-study notation (preclinical): transected Achilles tendon models have reported faster collagen organization versus controls when BPC-157 was administered systemically or locally.
  • Case-study notation (preclinical): muscle crush designs have associated BPC-157 with improved functional recovery markers and angiogenesis-related histology.
  • Clinical translation remains limited; published human interventional evidence is sparse compared with animal literature.
