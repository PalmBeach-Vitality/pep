SS-31

Mitochondria-Targeted Tetrapeptide

Scientific Overview

SS-31, also called elamipretide, is a synthetic tetrapeptide studied for association with cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane. It is a mitochondria-targeted research peptide, not a growth-hormone analog.

Mechanism focus: Reviews describe membrane binding, effects on mitochondrial readouts, and use in cellular stress models. Mechanism papers focus on the inner membrane rather than on endocrine receptors.

Research Use Cases

  • Mitochondrial membrane assays
  • Cardiolipin-interaction studies
  • Cellular stress models involving mitochondria
  • Comparison with other mitochondria-targeted peptides

Use-Case Study Notations

  • Case-study notation: reviews summarize elamipretide structure and proposed mitochondrial mechanism.
  • Case-study notation (preclinical): cell and mouse papers examine SS-31 in mitochondrial stress models.
  • MOTS-c is a different mitochondrial peptide. The two sequences are not interchangeable.
