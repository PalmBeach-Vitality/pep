GHK-Cu

Copper Tripeptide (Gly-His-Lys·Cu²⁺)

Scientific Overview

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide studied for extracellular-matrix remodeling, antioxidant gene programs, and skin/wound biology.

Mechanism focus: GHK chelates Cu²⁺ and is reported to modulate metalloproteinases, collagen/elastin-related gene expression, and anti-inflammatory transcriptional programs in skin and soft tissue models.

Research Use Cases

  • Dermal regeneration and cosmetic-peptide research
  • Wound-healing and scar-remodeling assays
  • Hair follicle and scalp biology models
  • Gene-expression studies of copper-peptide signaling

Use-Case Study Notations

  • Case-study notation: Pickart and colleagues documented GHK-associated tissue remodeling and gene resetting signatures in skin-aging contexts.
  • Case-study notation: copper-tripeptide complexes have been evaluated for attenuating inflammatory and fibrotic responses in lung injury models.
  • Formulation stability of the Cu²⁺ complex is an important analytical QC variable.
