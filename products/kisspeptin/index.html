Kisspeptin

KISS1 Neuropeptide

Scientific Overview

Kisspeptin refers to peptides encoded by KISS1 that activate the KISS1R receptor, also called GPR54. Research centers on how this signaling controls GnRH neurons and the reproductive endocrine axis.

Mechanism focus: Kisspeptin-KISS1R signaling is studied as an upstream input to hypothalamic GnRH release. Reviews cover receptor biology and reproductive neuroendocrinology rather than a single downstream pathway.
