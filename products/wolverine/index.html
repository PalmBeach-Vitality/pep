Wolverine Stack

BPC-157 + TB-500

Scientific Overview

Wolverine is a research stack pairing BPC-157 with a thymosin-β4–related fragment (TB-500 motif) to interrogate complementary soft-tissue repair and actin-migration pathways.

Mechanism focus: Combines angiogenesis/tissue-protection hypotheses from BPC-157 literature with actin-cytoskeleton motility biology from Tβ4 research.
